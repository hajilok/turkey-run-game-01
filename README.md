# FEATHERBREAK: The Great Thanksgiving Escape — 2.5D Illuminated 4-Stage Stealth-Action Turkey Runner

Outsmart **Farmer Barnaby**, vault roaring **425°F cast-iron & brick roasting ovens** reflecting across gleaming 2.5D perspective floors, weave through spinning **Damascus carving knives** and chopping cleavers, slip inside hollowed pumpkins and copper stockpots to vanish from his lantern beam, spring off bounce-hay bales onto high loft routes, lure Barnaby into **gravy slip traps**, detonate **flour-sack smoke blinds**, slam oven doors or bonk hanging copper pots with mouse-aimed acorns, and race **1,200 meters** from the **Golden Farmyard** through the **Harvest Barn**, **Applewood Pantry**, and **Roasting Kitchen** to the **Freedom Gate** across **Story Escape**, **Endless Harvest**, and **Chef's Frenzy (2x Score)** modes!

Built for the [`turkey-run-game-01`](https://make.fun/bounty/turkey-run-game-01?ref=41fw6r6c) competition on [make.fun](https://make.fun/r/41fw6r6c).

## Controls
- **Move**: `A` / `D` or `←` / `→`
- **Jump & Feather-Flutter**: `W` / `↑` / `Space` (hold in mid-air to glide; bounce off hay bales to reach upper loft plumes)
- **Duck / Hide in Cover**: `S` / `↓` / `E` inside pumpkins, haystacks, flour sacks, or copper stockpots
- **Gobble-Dash**: `Shift` or `Right-Click` (invulnerable burst + Close-Call graze combo)
- **Toss Harvest Acorn**: Aim & `Left-Click` (distract Farmer Barnaby, burst flour sacks, drop hanging pots, or slam oven latches)
- **Pause / Mute**: `P` or `Esc` to pause, `M` to toggle procedural Web Audio bluegrass soundtrack & SFX

## Play Locally

```bash
node serve.ts

```
